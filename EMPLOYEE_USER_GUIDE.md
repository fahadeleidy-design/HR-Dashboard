## Chapter 1: Welcome

- Access your personal information
- Request leave and time off
- Submit expense claims
- View payslips and compensation details
- Track your attendance
- Enroll in training programs
- Manage your documents
- Update your personal information

As an employee, you have access to:

✅ **View** your personal information
✅ **Request** leave and time off
✅ **Submit** expense claims and loan requests

✅ **Enroll** in training programs
✅ **Upload** personal documents

❌ **Cannot** approve requests (manager function)

## Chapter 3: Leave Management

### 3.1 Understanding Leave Types

#### 3.1.1 Annual Leave
- **Entitlement**: 21 days per year (30 days after 5 years)
- **Payment**: 100% of salary
- **Approval**: Manager approval required
- **Notice**: 3 days advance notice recommended
- **Maximum**: 30 consecutive days

#### 3.1.2 Sick Leave
- **Entitlement**: 120 days per year
- **Payment**:
  - First 30 days: 100% salary
  - Next 60 days: 50% salary
  - Last 30 days: Unpaid
- **Certificate**: Required after 3 consecutive days
- **Approval**: Manager approval + Medical certificate

#### 3.1.3 Hajj Leave
- **Entitlement**: 10 days
- **Frequency**: Once per 5 years
- **Payment**: 100% of salary
- **Approval**: Manager approval required
- **Documentation**: Proof may be required

#### 3.1.4 Emergency Leave
- **Entitlement**: Variable (manager discretion)
- **Payment**: Variable
- **Approval**: Manager approval required
- **Use**: Urgent family matters

#### 3.1.5 Other Leave Types
- **Maternity Leave**: 70 days (full pay)
- **Paternity Leave**: 3 days (full pay)
- **Bereavement Leave**: 3 days (full pay)
- **Unpaid Leave**: By arrangement

#### 3.3.1 Common Validation Errors

❌ **"Insufficient leave balance"**
- You don't have enough days available
- **Solution**: Reduce days requested or choose different leave type

❌ **"Overlapping leave exists"**
- You have approved leave during selected dates
- **Solution**: Select different dates

❌ **"Start date cannot be in the past"**
- Selected date has passed
- **Solution**: Choose a future date

❌ **"End date must be after start date"**
- Dates are reversed
- **Solution**: Correct the date order

💡 **Tips**:
- Request leave at least 3 days in advance
- Check team calendar for blackout dates
- Attach medical certificate for sick leave > 3 days
- For urgent leave, contact manager directly first

## Chapter 4: Expense Claims

### 4.1 Understanding Expense Claims

**What are expense claims?**
Expenses you paid for business purposes that the company will reimburse.

**Eligible Expenses:**
✅ Business travel (flights, hotels, car rental)
✅ Client entertainment
✅ Office supplies
✅ Mobile phone bills (if applicable)
✅ Mileage (personal car for business)
✅ Training materials
✅ Other pre-approved business expenses

❌ **Not Eligible:**
- Personal expenses
- Commuting to/from office
- Personal meals
- Expenses without receipts
- Non-approved purchases

### 5.2 Understanding Your Payslip

#### 5.2.1 Earnings Section

**Basic Salary**: Your fixed monthly salary
**Housing Allowance**: Housing benefit (typically 40-50% of basic)
**Transportation Allowance**: Travel benefit
**Other Allowances**: Mobile, food, other benefits
**Overtime**: Extra hours worked (1.25× hourly rate)
**Bonuses**: Performance or annual bonuses

**Gross Salary** = Sum of all earnings

#### 5.2.2 Deductions Section

**GOSI (Employee)**: 10% of (Basic + Allowances)
- Your social insurance contribution
- Maximum base: 45,000 SAR

**Loan Installment**: Monthly loan payment (if applicable)

**Advance Deduction**: Salary advance repayment (if applicable)

**Absence Deduction**: Unpaid leave or unauthorized absence

**Other Deductions**: Penalties, equipment, etc.

**Total Deductions** = Sum of all deductions

#### 5.2.3 Net Salary Calculation

```
Net Salary = Gross Salary - Total Deductions

Example:
  Gross:      9,000 SAR
  Deductions:   550 SAR
  ───────────────────────
  Net:        8,450 SAR
```

**This is the amount deposited to your bank account.**

1. **Check credentials**
   - Username: Your email or employee number
   - Password: Case-sensitive
   - Caps Lock: Make sure it's OFF

2. **Reset password**
   - Click **Forgot Password?** on login page
   - Enter your email
   - Check email for reset link
   - Create new password

3. **Account locked**
   - After 5 failed attempts, account locks for 30 minutes
   - Wait 30 minutes and try again
   - Or contact IT Helpdesk to unlock immediately

4. **Still can't log in?**
   - Contact IT Helpdesk: support@yourcompany.com
   - Provide: Employee number, full name

#### Issue: Leave Request Shows "Insufficient Balance"

**Symptoms:**
- Cannot submit leave request
- Error: "Insufficient leave balance"

**Solutions:**

1. **Check your balance**
   - Go to Leave → My Balances
   - Verify available days

2. **Reduce requested days**
   - Request fewer days
   - Split into multiple requests

3. **Balance calculation**
   - Remember: Leave accrues monthly (1.75 days/month for annual)
   - New employees: Wait for accrual

4. **Contact HR**
   - If balance seems incorrect
   - If you believe there's an error

#### Issue: Payslip Not Showing

**Symptoms:**
- Missing payslip for current month
- "No payslips found" message

**Solutions:**

1. **Check payroll date**
   - Payslips typically available on 27th of each month
   - May be delayed if holidays

2. **Verify employment status**
   - Only active employees receive payslips
   - Check with HR if recently joined

3. **Clear browser cache**
   - Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Windows)
   - Cmd+Shift+Delete (Mac)
   - Select cached images and files
   - Clear and refresh page

4. **Contact Finance**
   - If payslip should be available but isn't
   - Email: finance@yourcompany.com

#### Issue: Expense Claim Rejected

**Symptoms:**
- Claim status shows "Rejected"
- Reimbursement not received

**Solutions:**

1. **Read rejection reason**
   - Open claim details
   - Review manager's comments

2. **Common rejection reasons:**
   - Missing receipt
   - Amount exceeds policy limit
   - Ineligible expense category
   - Unclear description

3. **Resubmit corrected claim**
   - Fix the issue
   - Add missing documentation
   - Resubmit for approval

4. **Contact manager**
   - Discuss rejection reason
   - Clarify any confusion

## Appendix B: Glossary

**Annual Leave**: Paid time off for vacation (21-30 days/year)
**Approver**: Person who reviews and approves your requests
**Attendance**: Daily check-in/check-out records
**Dashboard**: Your homepage showing key information
**GOSI**: General Organization for Social Insurance
**Grace Period**: 30-minute late allowance without penalty
**Gross Salary**: Total earnings before deductions
**Net Salary**: Amount paid after all deductions (take-home pay)
**Payslip**: Monthly salary statement
**Request**: Formal submission (leave, expense, etc.)
**Status**: Current state of a request (pending, approved, rejected)
**WPS**: Wage Protection System (bank salary transfer)
