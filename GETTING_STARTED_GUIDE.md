## Chapter 1: Welcome

### 1.1 Welcome to the Saudi HR Management System!

Congratulations on joining our organization! This guide will help you get started with our Human Resources Management System. Whether you're an employee, manager, or HR staff member, this system will be your daily tool for managing work-related tasks.

### 1.2 What is This System?

The Saudi HR Management System is a comprehensive platform that handles all HR-related processes including:

- **Employee Management**: Personal information, records, documents
- **Time Management**: Leave requests, attendance tracking
- **Compensation**: Payroll, salary information, benefits
- **Performance**: Reviews, goals, feedback
- **Development**: Training courses, skill development
- **Self-Service**: Submit requests, view information, manage profile

### 1.3 Benefits for You

✅ **24/7 Access**: View your information anytime, anywhere
✅ **Quick Requests**: Submit leave and expenses in minutes
✅ **Instant Updates**: Real-time notifications and status tracking
✅ **Paperless**: Digital documents, approvals, and records
✅ **Transparency**: Clear visibility into your requests and approvals
✅ **Mobile-Friendly**: Access from any device

### 1.4 System Requirements

**Supported Browsers:**
- Google Chrome (recommended)
- Mozilla Firefox
- Safari
- Microsoft Edge

**Devices:**
- Desktop computers
- Laptops
- Tablets
- Smartphones

**Internet Connection:**
- Required for all operations
- Minimum: 1 Mbps

💡 **Tip**: For best experience, use the latest version of Chrome or Firefox

## Chapter 2: System Overview

### 2.1 Key Features by Role

#### If You're an **Employee**:
- View personal information and documents
- Request leave and track status
- Submit expense claims
- View payslips and salary details
- Track attendance
- Enroll in training courses
- View performance reviews
- Update personal information

#### If You're a **Manager**:
- Everything an employee can do, PLUS:
- View team members and their information
- Approve leave requests
- Approve expense claims
- Conduct performance reviews
- Assign training to team members
- View team reports and analytics

#### If You're in **HR**:
- Everything a manager can do, PLUS:
- Manage all employee records
- Process leave requests company-wide
- Track compliance (GOSI, Nitaqat, WPS)
- Manage documents and renewals
- Handle recruitment processes
- Generate HR reports
- Configure leave types and policies

#### If You're in **Finance**:
- Process monthly payroll
- Generate WPS files for banks
- Approve expense reimbursements
- Manage loans and advances
- Handle GOSI contributions
- Generate financial reports
- Track budgets and cost centers

#### If You're an **Admin**:
- Configure system settings
- Manage user accounts and permissions
- Set up departments and structures
- Configure workflows
- Maintain system integrations
- Monitor system health

### 2.2 Common Terminology

**Dashboard**: Your home page showing key information and quick actions

**Module**: A major section of the system (Leave, Payroll, Attendance, etc.)

**Request**: A formal submission you make (leave request, expense claim, etc.)

**Approval**: When a manager or authorized person reviews and approves/rejects your request

**Status**: Current state of a request:
- **Pending**: Waiting for action
- **Approved**: Accepted
- **Rejected**: Denied
- **Completed**: Finished

**Notification**: Alert about important events or actions needed

**Payslip**: Monthly salary statement showing earnings and deductions

**GOSI**: General Organization for Social Insurance (Saudi social security)

**WPS**: Wage Protection System (electronic salary payment through banks)

**Nitaqat**: Saudi government program for workforce nationalization

## Chapter 5: Basic Tasks by Role

### 5.1 For Employees: First Essential Tasks

#### Task 1: View Your Information (2 minutes)

1. Click **My Info** in sidebar
2. Review your personal details
3. Check that all information is correct
4. Note any errors to report to HR

**What You'll See:**
- Name, employee number, job title
- Department and manager
- Contact information
- Hire date and employment status
- Bank account details

#### Task 2: Check Leave Balance (1 minute)

1. Click **Leave** in sidebar
2. Click **My Balances** tab
3. View your available leave days

**You'll See:**
- Annual Leave: X days
- Sick Leave: X days
- Other leave types
- Leave history

#### Task 3: View Your Latest Payslip (2 minutes)

1. Click **Payroll** in sidebar
2. Click **My Payslips** tab
3. Click **View** on the latest month
4. Review your earnings and deductions
5. Download PDF if needed

#### Task 4: Set Up Notifications (3 minutes)

1. Click your profile icon (top-right)
2. Select **Settings**
3. Go to **Notifications** tab
4. Choose your preferences:
   - ✓ Email notifications
   - ✓ In-app notifications
   - ✓ SMS (optional)
5. Save settings

### 5.2 For Managers: First Essential Tasks

#### Task 1: View Your Team (2 minutes)

1. Click **Team** or **Employees** in sidebar
2. Filter: **My Direct Reports**
3. Review your team members

**You'll See:**
- List of team members
- Their positions and status
- Quick contact information

#### Task 2: Check Pending Approvals (2 minutes)

1. Look for notification badge on dashboard
2. Click **Pending Approvals** or **Requests**
3. See what needs your approval:
   - Leave requests
   - Expense claims
   - Attendance corrections
   - Other requests

#### Task 3: Review Team Attendance (3 minutes)

1. Click **Attendance** in sidebar
2. Select **Team View** or similar
3. See today's attendance status
4. Note any absences or late arrivals

### 5.3 For HR Staff: First Essential Tasks

#### Task 1: Access Employee Directory (2 minutes)

1. Click **Employees** in sidebar
2. View complete employee list
3. Use search and filters
4. Note your access level (read/write)

#### Task 2: Check Compliance Dashboard (3 minutes)

1. Click **Compliance** in sidebar
2. View key metrics:
   - GOSI status
   - Nitaqat percentage
   - Document expiries
   - Pending items

#### Task 3: Review Pending Actions (3 minutes)

1. Check dashboard for alerts
2. Review pending leave requests
3. Check document renewals needed
4. Note any urgent items
