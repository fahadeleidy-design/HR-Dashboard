## Introduction

The Performance Management System is a comprehensive platform designed to manage employee performance, set and track goals, conduct reviews, gather feedback, and plan for succession. This system supports:

- **OKR & KPI Management**: Set objectives, key results, and key performance indicators
- **Continuous Performance Tracking**: Regular check-ins and progress updates
- **360-Degree Feedback**: Multi-rater feedback from peers, managers, and direct reports
- **Performance Reviews**: Structured review cycles with self and manager assessments
- **Succession Planning**: Identify and develop future leaders
- **Recognition Programs**: Acknowledge and reward exceptional performance
- **Calibration Sessions**: Ensure fair and consistent performance ratings
- **9-Box Talent Grid**: Plot employees by performance vs potential

## Goals Management

Goals Management allows employees to set, track, and achieve their objectives aligned with company strategy.

### Creating a New Goal

1. Navigate to the **Goals** tab
2. Click **New Goal** button (top right)
3. Fill in the goal details:

#### Basic Information
- **Goal Title**: Clear, concise description (e.g., "Increase Sales by 20%")
- **Description**: Detailed explanation of the goal
- **Type**: Select from:
  - **Objective**: High-level strategic goal
  - **Key Result**: Measurable outcome of an objective
  - **KPI**: Key Performance Indicator
  - **Personal Development**: Individual growth goal

#### Goal Settings
- **Priority**: Low, Medium, High, or Critical
- **Category**: Select from predefined categories (optional)
- **Target Value**: Numeric goal (e.g., 100)
- **Unit of Measure**: How to measure (e.g., %, count, SAR)
- **Weight %**: Importance relative to other goals (0-100%)

#### Timeline
- **Start Date**: When work begins
- **Due Date**: Deadline for completion
- **Notes**: Additional context or instructions

### Tracking Goal Progress

#### Progress Updates (Check-ins)

Regular check-ins keep goals on track and visible:

1. Click the **Update** button on any goal card
2. The check-in modal has two sections:
   - **New Update** (left): Record your progress
   - **Update History** (right): View past check-ins

3. Complete the check-in form:
   - **Progress %**: Current completion percentage (0-100)
   - **Current Value**: Actual achievement vs target
   - **Status Update**: What you've accomplished
   - **Blockers**: Obstacles preventing progress
   - **Support Needed**: Help required from others
   - **Next Steps**: Planned actions

4. Click **Save Update**

#### Automatic Status Updates

The system automatically updates goal status based on progress:

| Progress | Auto Status |
|----------|-------------|
| 0-49% | At Risk |
| 50-74% | Active |
| 75-99% | On Track |
| 100% | Completed |

### Goal Status Badges

Goals display colored status badges:

- **Draft** (Gray): Not yet started
- **Active** (Blue): In progress
- **On Track** (Green): Progressing well
- **At Risk** (Yellow): Needs attention
- **Behind** (Red): Significantly delayed
- **Completed** (Green): Successfully finished
- **Cancelled** (Gray): No longer pursued

### Editing Goals

1. Click the **Edit** icon (pencil) on a goal card
2. Modify any goal details
3. Click **Update** to save changes

**Note**: You can edit goals you created or goals for your direct reports (managers only).

### Deleting Goals

1. Click the **Delete** icon (trash) on a goal card
2. Confirm the deletion
3. The goal and all its check-ins will be permanently removed

**Warning**: This action cannot be undone. Consider cancelling the goal instead.

### Filtering Goals

Use the filter dropdowns to focus on specific goals:

**By Status**:
- All Status
- Active
- On Track
- At Risk
- Completed

**By Type**:
- All Types
- Objective
- Key Result
- KPI
- Personal Development

### Goal Card Information

Each goal card displays:
- Goal title and description
- Status and priority badges
- Category (if assigned)
- Progress bar with percentage
- Current vs target value
- Due date
- Action buttons (Update, Edit, Delete)

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews provide structured evaluation of employee performance.

### Review Types

The system supports multiple review types:

- **Self Review**: Employee self-assessment
- **Manager Review**: Direct manager evaluation
- **Peer Review**: Feedback from colleagues
- **360-Degree Review**: Comprehensive multi-rater feedback

### Review Process

#### As an Employee (Self-Review)

1. You'll receive a notification when a review cycle opens
2. Navigate to **Reviews** tab
3. Click on your pending review
4. Complete your self-assessment:
   - Rate your performance on key competencies
   - Evaluate goal achievement
   - Provide narrative responses
   - Highlight strengths and challenges
5. Submit the review by the deadline

#### As a Manager

1. Access direct reports' reviews from the **Reviews** tab
2. Review the employee's self-assessment
3. Complete your evaluation:
   - Provide ratings on competencies
   - Assess goal completion
   - Write detailed feedback
   - Identify strengths and development areas
4. Discuss the review with the employee (one-on-one meeting)
5. Finalize and submit the review

### Review Components

**Competency Ratings**
- Rate employees on defined competencies
- Compare self-ratings with manager ratings
- Provide comments for each competency
- Final rating determined through calibration

**Goal Achievement**
- Review all goals set during the period
- Rate achievement percentage
- Document outcomes and learnings
- Link performance to goals

**Open-Ended Questions**
- Strengths: What the employee does well
- Areas for Improvement: Development opportunities
- Development Goals: Future growth plans
- Additional Comments: Any other feedback

### Review Workflow

```
Draft → Self-Review → Manager Review → Calibration → Completed
```

1. **Draft**: Review created but not started
2. **Self-Review**: Employee completes self-assessment
3. **Manager Review**: Manager provides evaluation
4. **Calibration**: HR and leadership align ratings
5. **Completed**: Final review shared with employee

### Review Acknowledgment

After review completion:
1. Employee receives the final review
2. Review the feedback carefully
3. Acknowledge receipt by signing electronically
4. Add any comments or concerns
5. Schedule follow-up discussion if needed

## Review Cycles

Review cycles define when and how performance reviews occur.

### Cycle Types

- **Annual**: Once per year (most common)
- **Semi-Annual**: Twice per year
- **Quarterly**: Four times per year
- **Monthly**: Monthly check-ins

### Cycle Stages

Each cycle progresses through stages:

1. **Planning**: Setup period, goals set
2. **Self Review**: Employees complete self-assessments
3. **Manager Review**: Managers evaluate employees
4. **Calibration**: Leadership aligns ratings
5. **Completed**: Reviews finalized and shared

### Key Dates

**Start Date**: Cycle begins, goals can be set

**End Date**: Performance period ends

**Self Review Deadline**: Last day for self-assessments

**Manager Review Deadline**: Last day for manager evaluations

**Calibration Date**: When ratings are aligned

**Review End Date**: Final reviews delivered

### Creating a Review Cycle (HR/Admin)

1. Navigate to **Cycles** tab
2. Click **New Cycle**
3. Configure cycle details:
   - Cycle name (e.g., "2024 Annual Review")
   - Cycle type (Annual, Semi-Annual, etc.)
   - All key dates
   - Description and instructions
4. Click **Create Cycle**
5. The cycle starts automatically on the start date

### Managing Active Cycles

**View Cycle Progress**:
- Completion percentages
- Number of pending reviews
- Overdue reviews

**Send Reminders**:
- Automatic reminders before deadlines
- Manual reminders for late submissions

**Extend Deadlines**:
- Adjust dates if needed
- Notify affected employees

## 360-Degree Feedback

360-degree feedback provides comprehensive performance insights from multiple perspectives.

### Requesting Feedback

#### As an Employee

1. Navigate to **Feedback** tab
2. Click **Request Feedback**
3. Select feedback providers:
   - Your manager (required)
   - Peers (2-5 recommended)
   - Direct reports (if applicable)
   - Other stakeholders
4. Choose feedback questions or use default template
5. Set deadline for responses
6. Send requests

#### Relationship Types

- **Manager**: Direct supervisor
- **Peer**: Colleagues at similar level
- **Direct Report**: Team members you manage
- **Self**: Self-assessment
- **Other**: Clients, cross-functional partners

### Providing Feedback

When you receive a feedback request:

1. Check your notifications or **Feedback** tab
2. Click on the pending feedback request
3. Answer all questions honestly and constructively
4. Feedback is anonymous (except manager feedback)
5. Submit by the deadline

### Feedback Questions

Typical questions include:
- **Rating Questions**: 1-5 scale on competencies
- **Open-Ended**: Strengths and development areas
- **Specific Behaviors**: Examples of performance
- **Recommendations**: Growth suggestions

### Viewing Feedback Results

1. Results available after all responses collected
2. View aggregated anonymous feedback
3. Manager feedback separately identified
4. Use insights for development planning

### Best Practices for 360 Feedback

**When Requesting**:
- Choose diverse perspectives
- Select people who work closely with you
- Give adequate time for responses (2 weeks minimum)
- Explain the purpose and importance

**When Providing**:
- Be specific with examples
- Balance positive and constructive feedback
- Focus on behaviors, not personality
- Be timely with your response

## Succession Planning

Succession planning ensures business continuity by identifying and developing future leaders.

### Critical Roles

Identify roles critical to business success:

1. Navigate to **Succession** tab
2. Click **Add Critical Role**
3. Define the role:
   - Role title and department
   - Current incumbent (if any)
   - Risk level if vacant (Low/Medium/High/Critical)
   - Time to fill estimate
   - Required competencies
   - Key responsibilities

### Talent Pool

Build a pipeline of potential successors:

#### Adding Successors

1. Open a critical role
2. Click **Add to Talent Pool**
3. Select employee
4. Set readiness level:
   - **Ready Now**: Can assume role immediately
   - **Ready in 1 Year**: Needs minor development
   - **Ready in 2-3 Years**: Requires significant preparation
   - **Future Potential**: Long-term prospect
5. Document development needs
6. Add notes on strengths and gaps

#### 9-Box Grid

Plot employees by performance and potential:

**Performance Axis** (Current):
- 1: Below Expectations
- 2: Meets Expectations
- 3: Exceeds Expectations

**Potential Axis** (Future):
- 1: Low Potential
- 2: Medium Potential
- 3: High Potential

**9-Box Categories**:
- **Top Right (3,3)**: High Performer, High Potential (Stars)
- **Top Middle (2,3)**: Medium Performer, High Potential (Growth)
- **Top Left (1,3)**: Low Performer, High Potential (Enigma)
- And so on...

### Development Plans

Create individualized development plans:

1. Select an employee in the talent pool
2. Click **Create Development Plan**
3. Define the plan:
   - Plan title
   - Career aspiration
   - Development areas
   - Target position
   - Target timeframe
   - Status (Active/On Hold/Completed)

4. Add development activities:
   - **Training**: Courses, workshops, certifications
   - **Mentoring**: Pairing with senior leaders
   - **Project**: Stretch assignments
   - **Job Rotation**: Cross-functional experience
   - **Certification**: Professional credentials
   - **Other**: Custom activities

5. Set start and end dates for each activity
6. Track completion and document outcomes

### Succession Planning Reports

Generate reports to review:
- Critical role coverage
- Talent pipeline depth
- Development plan progress
- Readiness gaps
- Succession risks

## Recognition & Rewards

Recognize and celebrate employee achievements.

### Recognition Types

- **Peer Recognition**: Colleague appreciation
- **Manager Recognition**: Supervisor acknowledgment
- **Company Recognition**: Organization-wide awards
- **Customer Recognition**: External appreciation

### Creating a Recognition

1. Navigate to **Recognition** tab
2. Click **Recognize Someone**
3. Fill in recognition details:
   - Select employee
   - Recognition type
   - Category (e.g., Innovation, Customer Service, Teamwork)
   - Title (e.g., "Outstanding Project Leadership")

### Recognition Categories

Common categories include:
- **Innovation**: Creative solutions
- **Customer Service**: Client satisfaction
- **Teamwork**: Collaboration excellence
- **Leadership**: Guiding others
- **Quality**: Excellence in work
- **Initiative**: Going above and beyond

### Performance Bonuses

Link recognition to financial rewards:

1. Create a performance bonus record
2. Link to a review or recognition
3. Specify:
   - Bonus type (Annual, Quarterly, Project, Spot)
   - Amount and currency
   - Reason for bonus
   - Payment date
   - Status (Pending/Approved/Paid)
4. Submit for approval

### Recognition Wall

View all public recognitions:
- Filter by employee, date, or category
- Like and comment on recognitions
- Share celebrations with the team
- Export recognition reports

## Best Practices

### Goal Setting

**SMART Goals**:
- **S**pecific: Clear and precise
- **M**easurable: Quantifiable outcomes
- **A**chievable: Realistic and attainable
- **R**elevant: Aligned with company objectives
- **T**ime-bound: Clear deadlines

**Recommendations**:
- Set 3-5 goals per cycle (not too many)
- Mix short-term and long-term goals
- Align with team and company goals
- Include both performance and development goals
- Review and adjust quarterly

### Regular Check-ins

**Weekly or Bi-weekly**:
- Update goal progress
- Document blockers immediately
- Request support proactively
- Celebrate small wins

**Monthly**:
- Review overall progress
- Adjust goals if needed
- Discuss with manager
- Update priorities

### Performance Reviews

**For Employees**:
- Keep a performance journal throughout the year
- Document achievements with metrics
- Gather examples of impact
- Be honest in self-assessment
- Prepare questions for your manager

**For Managers**:
- Take notes throughout the review period
- Focus on behaviors and outcomes, not personality
- Provide specific examples
- Balance positive and constructive feedback
- Discuss development opportunities
- Follow up on action items

### 360-Degree Feedback

**Requesting**:
- Give 2 weeks minimum for responses
- Choose 5-8 feedback providers
- Include diverse perspectives
- Communicate the purpose

**Providing**:
- Be constructive, not critical
- Use specific examples
- Focus on recent behaviors
- Balance positive and developmental feedback
- Complete within deadline

### Succession Planning

**Proactive Planning**:
- Identify critical roles annually
- Build talent pipeline early
- Create development plans
- Rotate high-potentials through assignments
- Monitor progress regularly

**9-Box Grid Usage**:
- Update placements annually
- Use performance review data
- Calibrate across teams
- Focus development on high-potentials
- Address low performers

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### Cannot Create a Goal

**Issue**: "New Goal" button is disabled or not visible

**Solutions**:
- Verify you're logged in
- Check if you have an employee record
- Confirm you have the necessary permissions
- Contact HR if issue persists

#### Goal Progress Not Updating

**Issue**: Progress bar doesn't reflect check-in

**Solutions**:
- Ensure you clicked "Save Update"
- Check if progress percentage was entered correctly
- Refresh the page
- Clear browser cache
- Report to IT support if issue continues

#### Cannot See Team Goals

**Issue**: Only seeing own goals as a manager

**Solutions**:
- Verify your manager role assignment
- Check employee reporting relationships
- Confirm you're viewing the correct company
- Contact HR to verify permissions

#### Review Not Appearing

**Issue**: Expected review not showing in list

**Solutions**:
- Check if review cycle is active
- Verify you're included in the cycle
- Check review status (may be completed already)
- Look in "Completed" filter
- Contact HR for cycle inclusion

#### Feedback Request Failed

**Issue**: Cannot send 360 feedback request

**Solutions**:
- Ensure all required fields are filled
- Verify selected reviewers are active employees
- Check that deadline is in the future
- Confirm you have active review cycle
- Try again after refreshing

#### Missing Permissions

**Issue**: Cannot access certain features

**Solutions**:
- Verify your role assignment
- Check with HR for role clarification
- Ensure your employee record is complete
- Request role upgrade if needed
- Contact system administrator

### Error Messages

**"No active company found"**
- You may not be assigned to a company
- Contact HR to verify employment status

**"Employee record not found"**
- Your user account lacks employee link
- Contact HR to complete profile setup

**"Permission denied"**
- Feature requires higher access level
- Contact your manager or HR

**"Invalid date range"**
- Start date must be before end date
- Check date format
- Ensure dates are reasonable

### Performance Issues

**Slow Loading**:
- Check internet connection
- Clear browser cache
- Try different browser
- Report persistent issues to IT

**Data Not Saving**:
- Check internet connection
- Don't close window while saving
- Wait for success confirmation
- Check browser console for errors

### Getting Help

**In-App Support**:
- Click Help icon (?) for tooltips
- Access user guides from Help menu
- Watch video tutorials

**Contact Support**:
- HR Department: For policy and process questions
- IT Support: For technical issues
- Manager: For goal and review guidance
- System Administrator: For permissions and access

## Glossary

**Check-in**: Regular progress update on a goal

**Competency**: Skill, knowledge, or behavior required for effective performance

**Development Plan**: Structured approach to building employee capabilities

**KPI**: Key Performance Indicator - measurable value demonstrating effectiveness

**9-Box Grid**: Talent management tool plotting employees by performance and potential
